The whole idea

One order, two shapes

Sales invoice
Twinfield sends it

A document Twinfield can print, email and chase. Needs the invoicing module.

<salesinvoice>
  <invoicetype>FACTUUR</>
  <customer>1000</>
  <line> 2 × 49.50  VH  8000 </>
</salesinvoice>
Journal transaction
You send it

The bookkeeping only. Works on every Twinfield subscription.

<transaction>
  <code>VRK</>
  detail  8000  credit   99.00
  total   1300  debit   119.79
</transaction>

Twinfield’s invoicing module is a separate product — shops that send their own invoices need the journal, not the document.

knows first

A bank statement is matched to an invoice in Twinfield — so for transfers and on-account orders, Twinfield knows before Craft does.

  • Reads the open value and match status back
  • Moves the order’s status, or records a Commerce payment

When it goes wrong

The reason,
not just the
refusal

Twinfield reports rejections inside a 200 OK, as attributes on the tag it disliked. The response body is the only place the reason ever appears — so Twinsies keeps it.

  • Request and response XML, access tokens redacted
$ php craft twinsies/auth/test
Connected to Bakkerij Jansen BV (001).
$ php craft twinsies/sync/order 2026-0448
Posted 2026-0448 as VRK 202600512.
$ php craft twinsies/sync/order 2026-0449
Failed: customer: Unknown dimension
$ php craft twinsies/reconcile/run
Checked 50, 7 newly paid, 0 errored.
